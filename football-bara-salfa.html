How to play

Pass the phone between each action.

  1. Both managers start with a budget of 100M$.
  2. One player appears at a time — you never see who comes next.
  3. Every auction opens at 0M$.
  4. Type any amount you want. There are no fixed steps.
  5. Your bid must beat the current bid and fit inside your budget.
  6. Pass whenever you want to stop bidding.
  7. Whoever passes loses the player. The rival pays the last bid.
  8. The manager who passed signs a secret player of the same position — for free.
  9. The secret player is revealed to both of you, then leaves the pool forever.
  10. Twelve auctions: 11 players and 1 coach.
  11. The squad lines up in a 4-3-3 and team power decides the final match.
